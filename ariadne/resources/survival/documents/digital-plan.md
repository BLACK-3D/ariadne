# Offline knowledge and digital continuity plan

Keep the local assistant, records, backups and reference media usable offline.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Keep the full Ariadne folder together with the intended local model and reference assets.
- [ ] Record startup instructions and where the owner pairing key is stored securely.
- [ ] Verify backup hashes and practice restoring a separate test copy without replacing live owner state.
- [ ] Keep a printed index of essential contacts and documents for times when the computer cannot run.
- [ ] Use a measured power budget, suitable backup hardware and manufacturer shutdown instructions.
- [ ] Keep private databases, books, medical records, keys and credentials out of public distribution packs.
- [ ] Record software versions, model provenance and document review dates.
- [ ] Test local search and document access without depending on a hosted service; record the scope and limits of the test.
