# Workshop tools and maintenance register

Inventory maintainable hand tools, measuring equipment, manuals and training.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Inventory tools, consumables, spare parts, compatible fasteners and storage locations.
- [ ] Keep manufacturer manuals, inspection requirements and safe operating limits with each tool.
- [ ] Record who has practical training and which tasks require a qualified specialist.
- [ ] Check measuring instruments against a suitable reference and record units explicitly.
- [ ] Separate damaged or unverified equipment from serviceable equipment.
- [ ] Plan maintenance intervals and replacement parts from actual use and manufacturer guidance.
- [ ] Record the power, ventilation, guarding and protective equipment needed for each work area.
- [ ] Use drawings and prototypes for review before committing materials to an unfamiliar build.
