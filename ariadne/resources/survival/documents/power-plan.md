# Essential power and backup planning card

Measure essential loads and plan safe backup power and equipment maintenance.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] List essential devices, measured watts, expected daily run time, surge requirements and charging options.
- [ ] Separate life-critical medical equipment plans from convenience loads and consult its provider.
- [ ] Record battery capacity, charging losses and actual observed runtime instead of assuming a universal runtime.
- [ ] Keep the generator manual and original CDC carbon-monoxide document with the power plan.
- [ ] Arrange qualified review of transfer equipment, wiring, fuel storage and any fixed installation.
- [ ] Inventory tested alarms, appropriate cables and manufacturer-required maintenance supplies.
- [ ] Record fuel quantities, inspection dates, operating limits and responsible trained users.
- [ ] Practice a controlled outage drill, document results and revise the load plan.
