# Shelter and evacuation planning card

Document safe places, access needs, evacuation choices and essential supplies.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] List home, nearby and out-of-area meeting places with addresses and contact information.
- [ ] Record local evacuation and shelter information from the responsible authorities.
- [ ] Identify transport, accessibility, mobility, language and pet needs for every household member.
- [ ] Prepare a portable document list and weather-appropriate clothing and bedding inventory.
- [ ] Identify hazards that require leaving rather than attempting a repair.
- [ ] Arrange a way to confirm a destination is open before departure when communications allow.
- [ ] Assign who carries supplies, checks on household members, and reports arrival.
- [ ] Practice the route and record changes, obstacles and alternate meeting points.
