# Materials inventory and reuse assessment

Track safe material identification, compatibility, storage and traceable reuse.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Record material type, quantity, dimensions, condition, source and any known specification.
- [ ] Keep labels and safety data sheets with chemicals and commercial materials.
- [ ] Mark unidentified, contaminated, damaged or degraded materials as unsuitable until assessed.
- [ ] Separate incompatible storage categories according to verified labels and professional guidance.
- [ ] Record whether a proposed reuse is decorative, temporary or load-bearing.
- [ ] Use qualified review for structural, pressure, electrical, potable-water and food-contact uses.
- [ ] Log test methods, measurements and uncertainty instead of treating appearance as proof of suitability.
- [ ] Keep a replenishment and disposal plan with local legal and environmental requirements.
