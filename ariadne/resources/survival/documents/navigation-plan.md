# Local map and route planning card

Prepare dated local maps, routes, place names and travel decisions.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Select maps for the actual area of interest and record source, scale, datum and publication date.
- [ ] Keep printed route maps and an offline copy that opens without a network connection.
- [ ] Mark meeting places, known shelters and essential services only from verified information.
- [ ] Plan alternatives for blocked roads, flood areas, accessibility needs and transport limitations.
- [ ] Record how coordinates are formatted and check them against recognizable landmarks.
- [ ] Practice using map and compass with competent instruction before relying on them.
- [ ] Treat bridge status, road access and weather as changing information that an old map cannot verify.
- [ ] Carry a travel and check-in plan appropriate to the household and local advice.
