# Garden and growing-system planning card

Maintain a site-specific growing plan, observations, seed inventory and local expertise.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Record available space, sun exposure, water access, soil observations and local growing conditions.
- [ ] Keep seed variety, source, lot, storage and planting records.
- [ ] Consult local extension guidance for region-appropriate crops, planting periods and soil testing.
- [ ] Start with a manageable trial and record yields, losses, labor, water and inputs.
- [ ] Plan crop rotation, soil cover and pest monitoring using sources appropriate to the site.
- [ ] Keep livestock welfare, veterinary needs and legal requirements in a separate plan if animals are involved.
- [ ] Separate tested food-preservation procedures from garden-production notes.
- [ ] Do not use historical farm literature as a substitute for current chemical labels or local food-safety guidance.
