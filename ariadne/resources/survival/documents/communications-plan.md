# Household and community communication plan

Maintain contacts, agreed messages, radio documentation and fallback methods.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Keep printed local and out-of-area contact lists and meeting-place details.
- [ ] Choose check-in times, a primary contact and a backup contact.
- [ ] Prepare a short message format for identity, location, needs and next check-in time.
- [ ] Inventory radios, chargers, batteries, cables and written operating instructions.
- [ ] Record lawful frequencies, licensing requirements and trained operators for transmitting equipment.
- [ ] Plan an accessible communication method for people who cannot use the primary method.
- [ ] Maintain a paper message log with time, sender, recipient and confirmation status.
- [ ] Test the plan periodically and distinguish confirmed information from rumors.
