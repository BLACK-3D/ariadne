# Hygiene and waste continuity plan

Plan hygiene, waste separation and lawful disposal when normal services fail.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Inventory handwashing, personal hygiene, cleaning and waste-storage supplies.
- [ ] Separate drinking-water containers from cleaning, wastewater and waste containers.
- [ ] Record household access needs and private facilities for children and dependent adults.
- [ ] Record municipal or local health department instructions for interrupted sewage and waste collection.
- [ ] List approved disposal arrangements and who is responsible for contacting service providers.
- [ ] Keep manufacturer labels and original agency cleaning guidance with supplies.
- [ ] Avoid mixing cleaning chemicals or treating a household plan as approval for an untested sewage system.
- [ ] Schedule cleaning, supply checks and documentation of unresolved contamination concerns.
