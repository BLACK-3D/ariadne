# Supplies, transport and replenishment register

Track quantities, dependencies, transport, responsibilities and replenishment.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Create an inventory with item, unit, quantity, location, owner and last verification date.
- [ ] Separate minimum requirements, preferences and assumptions in each plan.
- [ ] Use explicit units and measured consumption when estimating how long supplies last.
- [ ] Record suppliers, alternates, lead times, transport needs and access constraints.
- [ ] Assign inspection, rotation, maintenance and replenishment tasks.
- [ ] Track shared resources, commitments, receipts and handovers without exposing private credentials.
- [ ] Run a small exercise and compare predicted needs with actual use.
- [ ] Revise the plan after household changes, expired supplies or failed assumptions.
