# Repair and construction readiness checklist

Document condition, requirements, approvals and professional review before work.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Record the problem, photographs, measurements, location and immediate access restrictions.
- [ ] Separate temporary shelter needs from permanent repair or rebuilding work.
- [ ] Identify utilities, permits, building-code requirements and responsible authorities.
- [ ] Arrange qualified inspection for structural damage, foundations, wiring, gas, pressure or fire systems.
- [ ] List verified materials, tools, costs, dependencies and required skills.
- [ ] Keep design drawings, assumptions, revisions and inspection outcomes together.
- [ ] Do not treat a generated CAD drawing or quantity estimate as engineering certification.
- [ ] Record acceptance criteria and obtain required inspections before putting the work into service.
