# Health continuity and care coordination card

Organize personal medical preparedness, contacts, training and continuity of care.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Keep an owner-controlled list of clinicians, emergency contacts, care providers and preferred facilities.
- [ ] Record prescriptions, original labels and storage requirements as directed by the prescriber or manufacturer.
- [ ] Ask the treating clinician about continuity plans for medicines, refrigeration and powered medical devices.
- [ ] Inventory first-aid supplies and record expiry dates and completed hands-on training.
- [ ] Document allergies, accessibility requirements and consent for sharing medical information.
- [ ] Plan transport, caregiver backup and communication support.
- [ ] Use qualified medical help for diagnosis, treatment and medication changes; this card contains no treatment protocol.
- [ ] Keep private medical records out of shared exports and general public reference packs.
