# Mutual aid and community coordination card

Coordinate needs, accessible communication, roles and accountable decisions.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Ask participants what support they need and what they can safely offer.
- [ ] Document consent, accessibility, privacy and safeguarding expectations.
- [ ] Assign clear roles for coordination, supplies, communication and record keeping.
- [ ] Maintain a current resource directory with responsible contacts and verification dates.
- [ ] Agree how requests are prioritized, confirmed, handed over and closed.
- [ ] Use existing local emergency coordination arrangements where available.
- [ ] Keep records of decisions, commitments, donated goods and unresolved needs.
- [ ] Review the plan with participants and avoid presenting an informal group as a public authority.
