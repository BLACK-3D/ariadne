# Food supply and cold-storage plan

Inventory, rotate and plan safe storage and preparation without inventing preservation recipes.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Record food quantities, lot identifiers, expiry dates, storage locations and packaging condition.
- [ ] Group foods by dietary needs, allergens, preparation requirements, and available cooking equipment.
- [ ] Plan menus using foods the household already eats; record the assumptions behind any duration estimate.
- [ ] Keep refrigerator and freezer temperature records and an outage start-time log.
- [ ] Use the original FoodSafety.gov outage chart for item-specific decisions rather than a generated table.
- [ ] Keep tested preservation instructions with their exact edition, ingredient quantities and equipment requirements.
- [ ] Create rotation, pest inspection and replenishment tasks with named owners.
- [ ] Keep infant feeding, medical diets and pet food requirements visible as separate inventory requirements.
