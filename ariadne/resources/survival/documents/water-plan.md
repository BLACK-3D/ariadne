# Household water continuity plan

Plan stored supplies, access, containers and source-specific safety decisions.

Original Ariadne planning card, checked 2026-10-28. This card organizes observations, responsibilities and resources; technical, clinical and installation procedures remain with their cited original sources and qualified practitioners.

- [ ] Record household members, pets, accessibility needs, planned duration, and the source used for each supply assumption.
- [ ] Inventory sealed drinking-water supplies by container, quantity, location, purchase date and rotation date.
- [ ] List normal, backup and emergency collection sources; mark unverified sources as unverified.
- [ ] Keep the original CDC water documents available offline and printed with the plan.
- [ ] Record local water authority and health department contacts and a place to log advisories.
- [ ] Keep chemical contamination questions separate from microbial treatment; do not let a generic treatment plan certify an unknown source.
- [ ] List containers, labels, cleaning supplies, and who will inspect and rotate each stored supply.
- [ ] Log measurements, observations and unresolved questions before making a source-specific decision.

References:
