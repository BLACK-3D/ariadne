OFFLINE VOICE

Say “Ariadne” once

Turn on the PC microphone, say “Ariadne”, then ask follow-up questions without repeating her name. Recognition stays on this server and makes no idle language-model calls.

Pair this tab to see the PC microphone state.

“Go to sleep” returns to wake-word listening while the microphone stays on. “Stop listening” or “Ariadne, shutdown” turns off PC microphone capture and startup listening. These controls do not shut down the PC.

The PC microphone works while this server is running, even with the browser closed. Without the startup option, a session lasts eight hours. Ariadne returns to waiting for her name after the conversation idle timeout. A wake word is not speaker authentication; nearby speech can issue requests.
