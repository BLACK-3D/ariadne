# Survival and rebuilding reference pack

Checked October 28, 2026. The machine-readable inventory is [catalog.json](../ariadne/resources/survival/catalog.json). It contains 16 independent planning domains, 16 original Ariadne planning cards, five unchanged official PDFs, and five reference-only entries. All 21 bundled files have their actual byte count and SHA-256 in the catalog, totaling 6,444,232 bytes. The PDF files contain 36 pages altogether. No startup download is required by these assets.

The domains are water, food, shelter, sanitation, health, power, agriculture, tools, materials, communications, navigation, construction, community, education, logistics, and digital continuity. This organization is an original planning taxonomy, not a commercial book's table of contents. The cards organize inventories, observations, responsibilities, assumptions and sources. They do not provide medication dosages, clinical treatment protocols, improvised chemical recipes, structural certification or electrical installation instructions.

## Rights and attribution

The original Ariadne planning cards use the repository's MIT License, copyright 2026 Ariadne contributors. The MIT license does not relicense the agency documents.

FEMA and FoodSafety.gov materials use the U.S.-government-work basis under [17 U.S.C. 105](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#105), subject to any separate third-party rights. No third-party copyright restriction was observed in the five selected PDF texts. Government publication or free download alone is not treated as proof that every item on an agency website may be redistributed. U.S. status is not a guarantee of unrestricted rights in every jurisdiction.

The [CDC agency-materials policy](https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html) permits reuse of most public-domain agency information, while identifying contractor/grantee and other exceptions. This pack preserves substantive content and identifies CDC/HHS as the source. **These CDC materials are also available free on the CDC website. Ariadne and this reference pack are not endorsed by CDC, ATSDR, HHS, FEMA, DHS, USDA, FoodSafety.gov, GPO, or the U.S. Government.** Agency names and marks in the unchanged documents identify the original source; no agency logo is adopted as Ariadne branding. Third-party images, separate media, and use of agency marks for promotion require their own review. CDC also recommends checking for revisions when materials are reposted.

## Reference-only resources and exclusions

- [Ready.gov emergency supply checklist](https://www.ready.gov/sites/default/files/2024-05/ready_supply-kit-checklist.pdf): the official host returned HTTP 403 during this build. The link is recorded; no mirrored bytes or unverified substitute are included.
- [USDA/NCHFP Complete Guide to Home Canning, 2015 revision](https://nchfp.uga.edu/resources/category/usda-guide): official [Introduction](https://nchfp.uga.edu/papers/guide/INTRO_HomeCanrev0715.pdf) and [Principles](https://nchfp.uga.edu/papers/guide/GUIDE01_HomeCan_rev0715.pdf) URLs were verified. The full guide's contributor-material redistribution rights were not separately cleared for this pack. Tested recipes, processing tables and equipment requirements are not rewritten into generated checklists.
- [NRCS Soil Health Educators Guide](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/soil/soil-health/soil-health-educators-guide) and [2021 Cropland In-Field Soil Health Assessment Guide](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-10/Cropland_InField_Soil_Health_Assessment_Guide.pdf): the assessment credits LandPKS, private contributors and European Commission material. Its whole PDF is not labeled public-domain or bundled. USDA's [NAL rights explanation](https://www.nal.usda.gov/web-policies-and-important-links) likewise distinguishes government information from protected third-party material.
- [Appropedia documentation license](https://www.appropedia.org/Appropedia:Intellectual_property/Documentation_licenses): content defaults to CC BY-SA 4.0, requiring attribution and share-alike for covered copies/adaptations; individual exceptions and embedded external media need separate checks. No Appropedia article body or media is bundled in this pack.
- [USGS topographic maps](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/national-geospatial-program/topographic-maps): a verified selection point for the owner's area. No large map download is implied. Each selected product's metadata, age, datum and credited data need review. The original tools/materials cards also link to [NIST SI Units](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/metric-si/si-units) as a measurement reference.

Historical and current sources can differ. For example, the 2017 EPA water handout and newer CDC guidance do not use identical numeric thresholds. The EPA handout was researched but is not bundled here. The application should show source dates, preserve original tables and avoid silently combining conflicting technical or health instructions. This pack supplies planning references, not live local alerts or a certification of water, food, medical care, equipment or construction.

Runtime use should open only the packaged files identified by the catalog. Future updates are deliberate provisioning work: check source and rights again, preserve the new original file, record its date/hash, and qualify it before changing the catalog. User books, private plans and medical information remain owner data and must not be folded into a public resource export. The existing 0.4 release ZIPs, checksums and delivery receipts were not modified by this new resource pack.
