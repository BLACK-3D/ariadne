## Frozen aggressive hypotheses

The exact preregistration predates outcome inspection. SHA-256: `01dc93f5799f58a6dc35c318ae5d5eecdfcc735be17ac0f7ec1671e58a943027`. All lookbacks exclude the current observation and require consecutive reviewed sessions.

1. **Shock breakout call:** VIX closes above the prior 20-session high and at least 10% above the previous close.
2. **Spike-fade put:** yesterday's VIX was at least 25; today's close falls at least 10% and below the prior five-session mean.
3. **Short-term stress call:** VIX9D/VIX exceeds 1.05 after five sessions at or below 1.00. Missing VIX9D blocks this hypothesis.

The quote backtester selects one long contract, 7–21 calendar days to expiry and within the current calendar month. Strike selection uses an actual individual **VX reference with exactly matching expiry**, 5–25% forward out of the money, nearest 14 DTE then 10% forward OTM. Spot VIX is never a forward-price substitute. Entries use a later ask, exits a later bid, with observed side sizes and spread checks. Each entry costs at most $125 including premium, entry fee and reserved exit fee; the research portfolio shares one $500 monthly allowance without profit recycling. Separate single-strategy counterfactuals must never be summed or adopted as active funds. A doubled-fee scenario uses $1.30 per side versus the $0.65 assumption, not a claim about actual index-option fees.

Missing mandatory-disposal evidence holds the entire research ledger, preserves the last dated mark and blocks month rollover. There is no fabricated expiry zero or settlement. Research reports retain coverage, rejected signals/quotes, fees, P/L excluding contributions, partial ledgers and assumptions. No winning-strategy claim is supported.

## VIX contract distinctions

[Cboe specifications](https://www.cboe.com/tradable-products/vix/vix-options/specifications) and [Cboe FAQs](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/vix/faqs) describe European exercise, cash settlement and the $100 multiplier. VRO is an AM special opening quotation, not closing VIX spot. The current preceding-business-day end-of-curb cutoff is represented explicitly in the imported official calendar. A proposed expiration-day extension marked **Effective TBD** is not enabled. Unsupported calendars, share delivery, American exercise, aliases and inconsistent contract identities are rejected. Supplied calendar/provenance assertions are structurally validated, not independently authenticated.

Actual historical calendars must begin on or after **25 April 2022**, the [official production curb launch](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/release_notes/2022/Schedule-Update-Production-Date-for-Cboe-Options-Implementation-of-New-Curb-Trading-Session.pdf). Older actual histories are rejected instead of applying today's cutoff retrospectively. Reviewed normal sessions end at 15:15 Chicago with curb through 16:00; [early-close sessions have no curb](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/schedule_update/2024/Cboe-Holiday-Reminder-Modified-Trading-Hours-on-Wednesday-July-3-and-Thursday-July-4-2024.pdf), so their last-trading cutoff is the early regular close itself. The distinct `vix-options-validation-v2` policy and research rule hash bind this correction without changing the frozen signal preregistration.

## Official index diagnostic study

The [official Cboe history page](https://www.cboe.com/tradable-products/vix/vix-historical-data) directly links VIX and VIX9D CSVs. These are **index levels, not option quotes**. A local study counts each fixed signal and describes subsequent one/five-session index changes, with a chronological descriptive split and no optimization. It does not report option P/L, probability of profit or tradable returns. Historical provider availability is unknown; current downloads cannot seed earlier paper decisions. Date-only rows retain `observed_at: null` rather than an invented timestamp.

The private 30 October download ends on **29 October 2026**, at VIX **17.20**, VIX9D **15.87**. The full retrospective study found **235 shock, 83 fade and 44 stress signals**; none triggered on the latest saved session. This is descriptive evidence only. It preserves original OPEN anomalies, reports calendar exclusions/missing sessions, uses CLOSE only, and fills no gaps. The 1999–2035 reviewed regular-session calendar does not certify extended-hours VIX execution.

Raw Cboe history stays in private local storage under [Cboe terms](https://www.cboe.com/terms). It is not redistributed in GitHub, source ZIPs or a commercial data service. No automatic index refresh is added; a new legally obtained CSV must be imported explicitly. The UI and future daily briefs display the saved source date and stale/missing-session status, even when offline.

## Local imports

Both actions require the authenticated owner, same-origin request, unpaused global/options/briefing controls, a fresh options revision and a unique request ID. They share existing operation ownership, uncertainty holds and the **existing 100-operation / 128 MiB evidence quota**. There is no automatic retry, deletion or quota increase. Completed evidence binds input/report SHA-256 and the frozen rule. Historical work never changes the active account.

`POST /api/options/vix-signals` and `POST /api/options/vix-backtest` accept exactly:

```json
{"request_id":"unique-local-id","expected_revision":7,"path":"market-data/vix/index-history.json"}
```

Paths must remain inside the configured Ariadne Workspace. For a signal study, the path is a small manifest:

```json
{
  "schema":1,
  "kind":"cboe_index_history",
  "files":[
    {
      "symbol":"VIX",
      "path":"market-data/vix/VIX.csv",
      "sha256":"REPLACE_WITH_EXACT_LOWERCASE_SHA256",
      "source_url":"https://cdn-api.cboe.com/api/global/us_indices/daily_prices/VIX_History.csv",
      "retrieved_at":"REPLACE_WITH_ACTUAL_AWARE_DOWNLOAD_TIME"
    }
  ]
}
```

Optionally add one VIX9D entry using its officially linked source URL and its own download time/hash. A VIX9D-only manifest, unknown field, duplicate symbol, changed CSV, external path or future retrieval is rejected. Each raw CSV is bounded at 2 MB, manifest at 64 KiB. Reports retain exact private input CSVs alongside the report/manifest; no implicit network call occurs.

For option research the path points to the strict `vix_index_options_history` JSON schema documented in `ariadne/vix_options.py`. It requires explicit calendar sessions/contracts, all three quote clocks, true bid/ask sizes and individual same-expiry VX references. Inputs are bounded at 10 MiB by the parser, 5,000 daily snapshots and 200 quotes per snapshot. Synthetic software fixtures are accepted only by the pure test engine and rejected by owner import. Missing real historical option data is a disclosed blocker, not replaced with index-derived prices.

Future daily brief JSON/Markdown and the bounded model-facing summary include VIX readiness, saved study dates and data gaps. Completed older briefs are immutable. The existing one-page brief remains compact and links the full report. Current source ZIP packaging includes code, rules, metadata and tests; it does not imply that the older complete bundled-model offline ZIP was rebuilt.
