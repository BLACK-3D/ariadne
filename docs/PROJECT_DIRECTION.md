# Ariadne — overall direction and daily innovation context

Updated November 1, 2026. This is project context, not runtime configuration or execution authority. Newer explicit owner instructions, recorded approvals, pauses and current risk controls take precedence.

## The overall idea

Build a useful, modular, Jarvis-like local assistant: an "AI in a box" that can keep working without Codex open, preserve useful capability without Internet, and run on locally powered equipment during an outage. It should understand requests, complete authorized work, connect useful systems and produce clear, reviewable results.

Optimize the finished system's recurring costs: model/API use, compute, memory, storage, energy, subscriptions and maintenance. Greater justified upfront engineering is welcome when it improves reliability or lowers measured operating cost. Keep voice interaction natural, fast and concise, and the interface intelligent, precise and presentable. These are continuing goals, not claims that every capability is complete.

## Current priorities

1. **Business value.** Compare and validate the quote/invoice handoff Service desk and print-on-demand apparel. Favor models that create value through coordination, automation or outsourced fulfillment without buying inventory or hiring a cleaning workforce. Develop real client intake, quotes, invoices, approval controls and repeatable delivery. Distinguish drafts and assumptions from customer evidence, collected revenue, fulfillment costs and net income. Seek sustainable net recurring value above operating costs; do not invent sales or a guaranteed profit target.
2. **Evidence-based trading research.** Improve usable free, no-account US stock/ETF options data, historical coverage, fixed strategy comparisons and paper accounting. Current work emphasizes stress-filtered puts and comparison with recovery/other hypotheses. The selected KO source is a partial, date-only EOD paper-price model; longer-history labels or a profitable example do not prove a profitable strategy. Preserve one shared **$500 monthly gross commitment / $100 all-in entry** limit, actual quote/size/clock checks, source holds and zero real orders. New funds, caps, brokerage access and strategy promotion require their existing authority; this overview grants none.
3. **Reliable offline autonomy.** Advance bounded agents and workflows, durable receipts, recovery after interruptions, local knowledge/tools, independent hosting and checked one-click portability. Preserve pauses, approvals and substantive-failure stops. Do not resume uncertain work merely to appear autonomous. Distinguish source-copy validation from a complete portable-model package or physical outage test.
4. **Interaction and local controls.** Improve concise conversational response, the selected British voice, wake-once/sleep interaction, clear daily briefs and useful system/device integrations. Microphone listening remains off until explicitly requested otherwise. Evaluate capability and latency using actual measurements, without turning isolated synthetic probes into universal guarantees.

## Once-daily updates and innovation

The existing 6 AM America/Chicago companion review should connect the completed local brief and dated repository observations to this direction:

- **Direction:** briefly state what we are building and which current priorities matter today.
- **Verified progress:** summarize actual recent project changes, completed receipts, paper results, business evidence, blockers and source freshness. Separate implemented/verified work, work in progress, research and future ideas. State when no new progress is verified.
- **Useful innovations:** identify up to three relevant opportunities from the existing saved capability watch and project evidence. Explain the benefit, offline suitability, recurring-cost implications or uncertainty, integration blocker and smallest useful next experiment. Prioritize practical value over novelty. A version change alone supports investigating a release, not claiming a specific new feature or installed capability.
- **Next step:** recommend one concrete action that advances an active priority, with an observable acceptance check. Keep recommendations separate from authorization to act.

Use the existing bounded daily repository collector; do not add another exhaustive scan or extra polling. Preserve original checked times and primary source links. If Internet is unavailable, use dated saved observations and clearly label stale or missing information. No automatic installs, upgrades, purchases, external messages, orders, model downloads, deployments or hardware activation follow from an innovation suggestion.

## Keeping the context accurate

Update this overview when the owner accepts a meaningful change in direction. Keep volatile facts in their actual reports and receipts rather than turning this document into an unverified status log. Read the current brief and runtime evidence before describing a feature as active or successful. Completed historical reports remain immutable.
