# Ford EOD paper experiment — 0.26.0

This workflow addresses the owner's clarified universe: US stocks/ETFs whose **underlying share price is below $100**, no data account, no brokerage connection and one shared $500 monthly options commitment allowance. It uses Ford, symbol F. A verified vendor regular-session stock reference for October 29, 2026 was $12.33; that dated observation is not a current quote.

## Source and evidence limits

[OnclickMedia's free plan](https://www.onclickmedia.com/pricing) advertises one year of historical EOD options and three years of stock data without an account. The [options documentation](https://www.onclickmedia.com/doc-options), [stock documentation](https://www.onclickmedia.com/doc-stock-data) and [snapshot metadata](https://www.onclickmedia.com/doc-meta-list) define the anonymous historical endpoints. Current/live access was denied during verification and is not used. No paid-field workaround, missing-strike reconstruction, account or subscription is added.

Actual historical responses contain date, expiration, strike, option type, bid, ask and quoted sizes. They **do not establish option quote-event timestamps**. Free chains may omit strikes; their warning and counts remain in private provenance. The stock reference uses unadjusted source labels for the exact source date: all 390 regular minute labels from 09:30 through 15:59, with an optional actually supplied 16:00 boundary bar; early closes use 210 labels through 12:59 plus optional 13:00. Duplicates, missing core labels and all other outside labels fail validation. The selected price is from the last actually supplied positive-volume bar, with its label and boundary presence retained. This is an explicit research source-label policy, not authentication of exchange event times, the official closing auction price or provider interval semantics. Daily rows labelled with next-day midnight are not relabelled. Synthetic minute option visualizations, last, mark, implied volatility and Greeks never determine fills.

The [provider overview](https://www.onclickmedia.com/doc-overview) and [About page](https://www.onclickmedia.com/About) describe research use and upstream data constraints. Original-provider rights, continuing availability, quote accuracy and a service-level guarantee have not been independently established. Raw market responses and private reports are excluded from GitHub/source distributions. This is private personal educational research; the software does not convey market-data redistribution or commercial-use rights.

This lower-evidence profile is separate from the original timestamped engines. Every snapshot and report states `research_only: true`, `execution_qualified: false` and `quote_observed_at: null`. EOD bid/ask are **modeled price proxies**. Positive side size does not prove liquidity was executable when a later decision was made. Standard 100-share deliverables are a model assumption; adjusted roots are rejected, but OCC deliverables are not independently qualified. No winning strategy or return is established by enabling collection or passing software tests.

## One account and one allowance

The owner migration acts on the existing healthy schema-2 options account. It requires no trades, fees, committed budget, position, pending decision or hold. Existing observations are allowed. Its account ID, cash, contributions, month and financial counters are retained exactly; migration adds **zero funding**. Original state, original forward data, old source configuration and migration witnesses remain checked and immutable. The old AAPL source is disabled atomically, with no automatic fallback or parallel funded book.

The shared $500 monthly cap covers premium, entry fee and a reserved exit fee. Gains do not replenish that allowance. Each modeled entry is limited to $100 all-in, one long standard contract, no short options, margin, exercise or real orders. The same-session underlying reference must be strictly below $100 at entry. Exits remain possible if it later rises above $100. A new month can add one $500 contribution only when the book is verified flat without pending work or holds; missed months do not receive catch-up deposits.

Three fixed hypotheses rotate rather than choosing the best historical result: recovery calls, breakout calls and breakdown puts. They use only the returned free candidate subset, 7–21 days to expiration, a bounded OTM range, positive uncrossed bid/ask, spread and side-size checks. The source captures the metadata expiry nearest 14 DTE plus any required held/pending expiry. Near month-end, that candidate can be outside the decision month and therefore ineligible even when another uncaptured expiry could qualify. This conservative subset can prevent entries; it is not evidence that no opportunity exists. A durable decision must precede the NY date of its modeled source-date fill. Actual receipt and decision times remain distinct from the source date. No decision is backdated into the historical sample.

A mandatory disposal session is committed at entry, before expiration/month-end. An exact held-contract bid is required. Missing required quotes, source-date gaps or unresolved disposal evidence hold the shared book rather than invent a sale, assume zero expiration value, create replacement positions or add funding. Legacy archive/reset controls cannot remove the active experiment or its migration lineage.

## Operation and brief

Open Venture Lab → options and inspect the Ford EOD source card. Migration requires explicit experimental/partial-data acknowledgments. New installations first create their one simulated portfolio; migration never creates a second allowance. An optional Workspace baseline manifest can seed up to 15 consecutive validated historical stock references. It is indicator warmup only, never historical option fills. Without a qualified baseline the engine waits for 14 prospective source observations before evaluating signals.

On each regular US exchange session, the enabled collector checks the previous completed source session after **9:35 AM Eastern**. This is a configured check time, not a vendor publication promise. A fixed child process has a short deadline, bounded raw bytes, fixed public host/paths, no cookies, credentials, proxy, redirects or retries. Parent and child verify current options, briefing, Internet and global-pause authority. The parent independently validates the returned raw evidence before publishing any state. Failed or uncertain work stops recurrence; reading the dashboard never collects, retries or trades.

The 6 AM brief reports the latest saved source date, actual receipt, daily proxy P/L, cash, fees, position/pending status, budget and source limitations. At 6 AM, the next 9:35 collection has not happened. No model calls are needed. With Internet unavailable, saved inspection and local tests remain usable, but no new prices are manufactured and no claims of live/offline market coverage are made.

The existing 100 evidence-operation / 128 MiB report bounds remain shared across the legacy and new workflow. No quota increase or automatic evidence deletion is introduced. Pausing or stopping collection does not resolve an open paper position; inspect its disposal/source requirements before changing configuration.

## Verification scope

Regression fixtures are explicitly generated software data, not provider market history. Tests cover capital conservation, non-recycling budgets, fees, causal decisions, month boundaries, missing quotes, artifact tampering, pause revocation, fixed transport and owner-only routes. Fresh-copy testing uses process-level network/model/owner-data guards. It does not imply a physical Wi-Fi outage test or a newly verified model-inclusive offline ZIP.

The fixed 14-session historical stock warmup acquisition returned HTTP 200 but failed the initial policy that required a 16:00 boundary bar. Its raw bytes and failed receipt remain unchanged. Read-only diagnosis found two dates with every regular 09:30–15:59 label but no extra 16:00 row. The revised policy above was separately reviewed against [NYSE core hours and closing auction boundaries](https://www.nyse.com/trade/trading-information), tested and registered as `onclick_regular_source_labels_v2`; provider interval semantics remain unverified. The original bytes can be deliberately requalified under that policy without a new request, repaired rows or historical option fills. Any resulting baseline has its own proof and manifest; the original failure is never overwritten. Without a qualified baseline, the engine warms prospectively. Published historical strategy failures remain visible; this source integration does not replace them with synthetic profitability.
